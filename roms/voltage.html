About

VoltageOS provides a sandboxed Google Play environment, restricting its access and privileges. This provides broad compatibility with the Google Play ecosystem while prioritizing privacy and security.


Your data, your rules. Along with monthly security updates for supported devices, VoltageOS enhances existing privacy touchpoints around the operating system and keeps users informed about how the system shares data.


Built on the open-source Android Open Source Project, VoltageOS aims to provide a pure Android experience free from unnecessary bloatware and vendor customizations.
