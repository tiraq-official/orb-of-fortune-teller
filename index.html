Right, hello.

I'm Tiraq — Saptak Maji on the official paperwork.

Student, tinkerer, and full-time resident of the Android rabbit hole. Most days I'm deep in the AOSP world — writing device trees, patching kernels, and figuring out why a build that worked yesterday now refuses to boot. It's a hobby and a mild obsession.
